# Not submitting: public terms review

Checked 2026-10-30. This is a reading of public documents, not a binding provider
determination or verification of the owner's private account/accepted offer.

## Findings and sources

- [Hackathon rules](https://nebiusglobalaihackathon.devpost.com/rules), sections
  4–6 and 11: submission requirements govern entry/judging. I found no stated
  fine, credit repayment or account ban solely for not submitting. Without an
  eligible entry, do not expect judging/prizes. Section 5 permits continued
  project updates in the Devpost portfolio after the submission period.
- [Builder Program terms](https://nebius.com/builders-terms-and-conditions),
  C/H/I: legitimate development, research, experimentation and learning are
  allowed; no project-completion/submission repayment condition found. General
  credits expire 90 days from issuance; account-specific expiry still matters.
  Unused credits may be forfeited on membership termination. The programme
  restricts abuse and production/revenue use beyond prototyping. Further
  usage after credit exhaustion/expiry can be pay-as-you-go.
- [Current Nebius agreement](https://docs.nebius.com/legal/agreement), 9/16:
  accrued fees and applicable termination/payment obligations persist. The
  hackathon ending does not itself cancel this separate services agreement.
- [Token Factory billing](https://docs.tokenfactory.nebius.com/other-capabilities/billing-new):
  negative balances trigger automatic card charging at the billing threshold
  or beginning of the month. This is usage billing, not a non-submission fine.
- [Legacy Token Factory terms](https://docs.tokenfactory.nebius.com/legal/terms-of-service)
  carry an important scope notice: for customers without the specified active
  September reserve, the updated Nebius agreement applies from September 28,
  2026. Do not base today's advice on the legacy text alone. Also reviewed the
  [current supplemental terms](https://docs.nebius.com/legal/token-factory).
- [Tavily terms](https://www.tavily.com/terms), 4: no general hackathon submission
  obligation found; actual subscription, renewal and usage obligations depend on
  the selected plan/order. Missing the hackathon does not cancel a paid plan.
- [LangSmith terms](https://www.langchain.com/terms-of-service), 2.5/3/9: no general
  hackathon submission obligation found; free access, paid fees and outstanding
  obligations have separate rules. Private promotion/order terms may differ.
- [Official resources](https://nebiusglobalaihackathon.devpost.com/resources) and
  [FAQ](https://nebiusglobalaihackathon.devpost.com/details/faqs) distinguish the
  direct Token Factory offer and Builder Program. Followed the public direct
  credit form; its contents require JavaScript and were not inspectable with
  the browsing tool. Account-specific form/email conditions remain a limitation.

## Interpretation for the owner

because an honestly undertaken prototype was not submitted. This is an inference
from those terms, not an express no-penalty promise from support. Registration,
credit redemption, service usage and paid subscriptions are distinct.

I did not inspect private billing, outstanding usage, subscriptions, special
offer emails or separate agreements. No conclusion of zero account balance,
zero charges or guaranteed future account eligibility follows from this review.
Toloka/Tendem-specific promotion terms were not independently verified here.
Prior project records of zero automated provider calls do not establish all
activity on the owner's service accounts.
